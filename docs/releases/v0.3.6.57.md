## Faster start
- **Uncharted 1 and 2 load their start-up files much faster.** While the game loads a big file at start-up (for
  example Uncharted 2's HUD, 25 MB), it waits in steps of 1 second for the graphics memory to be filled, three pieces
  at a time. On a PS4 the disc is slower than that, so the wait hardly happened; on a PC the file is read at once and
  the game sat through many of these 1-second steps. The step is now one PS4 frame (1/60 s). The loading order and
  everything the game loads stay the same.
- **Fast start, on by default:** the logo movie is skipped (as the old *Skip intro logos* option did, now without
  its black stand-in movie), and between the end of the shader preparation and the title screen the screen (still
  black) runs at 480 Hz, so the title's files, which load a few per frame, are done sooner. The normal frame rate
  is back as soon as the title shows.
- Measured from starting a game to its title screen on an RTX 5080 PC, EU 1.02, shaders already prepared:
  Uncharted 1 about 4.5 s sooner, Uncharted 2 about 4 s sooner (mostly the start-up files), Uncharted 3 about 1.2 s
  sooner (fast start: 1.7 s -> 0.5 s after the shader preparation). The times on your PC depend on the drive, the CPU and the shader
  cache. Loading a level from the menu is not changed.
- Everyone gets fast start once with this update, also if *Skip intro logos* was off before. To see the logos again:
  launcher, Game, untick *Skip intro logos*.

## Ultrawide
- **Cutscene videos switch to 16:9 from their first frame.** Before, the first half second of a video (and the black
  before it) was shown stretched across the ultrawide screen, then jumped to 16:9. The port now sees the game start a
  video and switches at once; after the video the picture fills the screen again from the first gameplay frame.
- **Uncharted 1: the flashlight no longer has a hard edge on ultrawide screens** (also at FOV 100; and at 16:9 with a
  FOV above 100). The ultrawide view was widening the flashlight's own projection as well, so its soft light pattern
  was cut off by the narrower light cone. Light cameras are now left alone; mirrored reflection views are still
  widened. Found and fixed by [Bazovsky](https://github.com/Bazovsky) in
  [PR #33](https://github.com/Mevlock/uncharted_pc-dev/pull/33).
