**Unofficial fork build.** The official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc); please don't report problems with this build to him. Everything in 0.3.6.38 plus the changes below.

**Installing:** the launcher of 0.3.6.30 or newer offers this update itself; or unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe`.

## New
- **Supersampling** (launcher, Display page, Render resolution; experimental): like NVIDIA's DLDSR or AMD's VSR, built into the port and working on any graphics card. The game renders at your screen's size (or 75% of it with the faster choice), the anti-aliasing (DLSS, FSR or XeSS) upscales that to 1.5 times the size on each side, and the port scales it back down to your screen with a sharp filter. Distant detail and thin edges come out cleaner.
  - Made for **1080p and 1440p screens** with graphics power to spare: on a 1080p screen distant scenery was about 10 to 15% more detailed in our tests and visibly crisper. On 3440×1440 and larger screens the difference is small.
  - Cost, measured on the developer's PC (RTX 5080, 16-thread Ryzen) in Uncharted 3: about 22% fewer frames on a 1080p screen (155 to 120) and about 11% at 3440×1440. On a weaker graphics card the cost is larger; try the faster choice first.
- **4K output** choices (Quality, Balanced, Performance; experimental): upscaled to 4K and scaled down to your screen. Best on 1440p 16:9 screens.

## Fixed
- **Uncharted 3 chapter 2, graphics card reset right after young Drake is thrown out of the museum** (reported on an Intel Arc A750): a shadow filter repeats a step as many times as a number in the game's data says; when that number was broken it ran billions of times and Windows reset the card. It is now capped far above any real count. Waiting for the player's confirmation.

## Changed
- **Pictures larger than the screen** (the 4K choices and the new ones) are now scaled down with a proper filter instead of one sample per pixel: sharper and without shimmer.
- The Render resolution list is wider so every choice is readable.

## For troubleshooting
- When the graphics card stops responding, the log can now name the shader it was stuck in: add `BB_GPU_BREADCRUMBS=1` in the launcher (Advanced, Environment). It slows the game down, so only use it when asked. The log also says which shader width (subgroup size) your card uses.
