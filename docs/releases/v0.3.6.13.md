**Unofficial fork build.** The official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc); please don't report problems with this build to him. This is SuperMedo's 0.3.6 plus this fork's changes; everything in 0.3.6.12 plus the list below.

**Installing:** unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe`. Your launcher settings carry over, but saves live in each install's own `user\savedata` folder: copy that folder over from your previous install, or set **Saves folder** in the launcher to it.

## New in 0.3.6.13
- **Trophies** (by drakolordx7): the games' own trophy lists, read from your game files (Uncharted 1: 54, Uncharted 2: 53, Uncharted 3: 59). When the game awards one, a notice with its icon shows at the top of the screen, and the launcher has a new **Trophies** page with a tab per game. Unlocks are kept with your saves. `BB_TROPHY_POPUP=0` hides the notice.
- **Xbox button prompts** (by drakolordx7, built on Umar's keyboard and mouse prompts): after you use an Xbox controller, or any controller that isn't a DualShock or DualSense, the games show Xbox buttons: A/B/X/Y in their colours, bumpers, triggers, sticks and d-pad. A PlayStation pad brings back the games' own pictures, the keyboard and mouse Umar's key pictures. The launcher's Controls page has a new **Button pictures** choice (automatic, keyboard, Xbox, PlayStation). The pictures are Kenney's Input Prompts (CC0).
- **Intel XeSS** as an anti-aliasing choice (by drakolordx7): Anti-aliasing > **Intel XeSS** in the launcher. It runs on any graphics card (best on Intel Arc); on NVIDIA and AMD cards it uses Intel's lighter model, which is softer than DLSS or FSR 4, so this release also gives it the launcher's **Anti-aliasing sharpness** (Medium or High suits it). Intel's `libxess.dll` comes with the build.
- **Uncharted 2's checkpoint crash fix no longer costs frame rate** (by drakolordx7): the fix for "ASSERTION: iSubMesh < m_numSubMeshInstances" (mostly just after a checkpoint save) used to wait for the graphics card each time the game read the results of its collision tests. It now hands them over without waiting, in all three games. Measured: Uncharted 2's museum about 97 FPS with it, 90 with the fix turned off; Uncharted 3's Cartagena square within 1–2% of having no fix at all.
- **Numpad keys can be bound** in the launcher's Controls page (by eduardoghi): they were saved as the top-row number keys, and numpad `+` and `*` not at all.

## Known issues
- Uncharted 3: single-frame lighting flashes are rarer since 0.3.6.7 but may not be gone entirely.
- Uncharted 1, opening boat deck: a black splash can flicker on a round hatch for single frames (it was there in every version of the port; the cause is known, the fix isn't done yet).
- Uncharted 1 and 2 with Quality, Balanced or Performance, and Uncharted 2 at native resolution, work in the opening areas but haven't had a full playthrough (cutscene videos, HUD, menus).
- Ultrawide: short scripted scenes can still switch to 16:9 by mistake; **Always fill** avoids it.
- Rarely, the game freezes while exiting; if it happens, end `bb-probe.exe` in Task Manager.
- Fast mouse aiming covers the EU (CUSA02343), US (CUSA02320) and EU/Russian (CUSA02344) versions; other versions keep stick-style aiming.
- DLSS Frame Generation goes up to 4×.
