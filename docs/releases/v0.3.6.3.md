**Unofficial fork build.** The official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc); please don't report problems with this build to him. This is SuperMedo's 0.3.6 plus this fork's changes; everything in 0.3.6.2 plus the list below.

**Installing:** unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe`. Your launcher settings carry over, but saves live in each install's own `user\savedata` folder: copy that folder over from your previous install, or set **Saves folder** in the launcher to it.

## New in 0.3.6.3
- **Quality, Balanced and Performance in Uncharted 1 and 2:** render smaller and let DLSS or FSR rebuild the full size, as in Uncharted 3. They draw their final picture and HUD with Uncharted 3's shaders, so the same upscaling works. Mainly for weaker graphics cards: on a fast GPU these games are limited by the CPU (Uncharted 2 museum: native ~100, Performance ~114 FPS).
- **Bloom on/off now works in all three games** (they share the effect).
- **Uncharted 1: sun shadows no longer shimmer** under DLSS, FSR and TAA: the shadow pass rebuilt positions from the depth without the sub-pixel jitter the scene had (from kj2223's fork).
- Launcher: clearer explanations for Render resolution and Anti-aliasing (DLSS is DLAA at Native in every game).

## Known issues
- Uncharted 1 and 2 with Quality, Balanced or Performance, and Uncharted 2 at native resolution, work in the opening areas but haven't had a full playthrough (cutscene videos, HUD, menus, frame generation).
- Ultrawide: short scripted scenes can still switch to 16:9 by mistake; **Always fill** avoids it.
