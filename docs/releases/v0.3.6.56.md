## Smoother motion on CPUs with 8 threads or fewer
- **The judder in Uncharted 1 (for example the chapter 7 jeep ride) is fixed**; the same fix applies to the other two
  games. On CPUs with 8 threads or fewer the port lets the game's idle worker threads sleep, to leave the
  CPU to the game. But when a job that was waiting for other work became ready again, the game never woke a sleeping
  worker (on the PS4 its workers never sleep), so the job and the frame waited until a worker's sleep ran out, up to
  10 ms. Uncharted 1 moves the world by the real time between frames, so the world moved in uneven steps at a steady
  60 FPS. The workers are now woken as soon as such a job is ready, in all three games.
- Measured at the start of Uncharted 1 chapter 7 with sleeping workers: frames more than 2 ms off went from 80% to
  about 1.5%, as smooth as with spinning workers, with no extra CPU use.
- PCs with more than 8 threads are not affected (their workers keep spinning, as before).

## Fixes
- **Uncharted 2 chapter 19 (Siege): Tenzin no longer jitters on the spot after the double door.** A bug in the original
  game: his combat AI sends him to a spot 1 metre across, but his walk counts as arrived a little further out, so he
  was sent there again every few frames. This one spot now accepts him once he has walked there; nothing else changes.
