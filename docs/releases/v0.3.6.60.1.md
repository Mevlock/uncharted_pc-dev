## Fix
- **Lower FPS and very high GPU usage since 0.3.6.59 on graphics cards that are near their limit.** The frame pacing
  added in 0.3.6.59 held each frame on the graphics card until its moment to be shown, and it kept the card busy while
  it waited: GPU usage stayed near 100% even with the frame rate capped (also in menus, loading screens and cutscenes),
  and on laptops and other cards working hard it cost frames, sometimes dropping below 60. This pacing is now off by
  default, so the GPU is free again. It comes back in a later release once it can wait without keeping the card busy.
  - If you liked the extra smoothness and your card has room to spare, `BB_EVEN_PRESENT=1` in the launcher's extra
    environment field turns it back on.
- **Correction to the 0.3.6.60 notes:** "Reserve CPU cores for rendering" is on the launcher's **Display** page, not
  under Performance.
