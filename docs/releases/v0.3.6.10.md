**Unofficial fork build.** The official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc); please don't report problems with this build to him. This is SuperMedo's 0.3.6 plus this fork's changes; everything in 0.3.6.9 plus the list below.

**Installing:** unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe`. Your launcher settings carry over, but saves live in each install's own `user\savedata` folder: copy that folder over from your previous install, or set **Saves folder** in the launcher to it.

## New in 0.3.6.10
- **Keyboard and mouse button prompts** (by Umar, see above): while you play on keyboard and mouse, the games' PlayStation button pictures show the keys and mouse buttons bound in `input.ini` instead, in Uncharted 1, 2 and 3 (hints, hold and tapping prompts, the title screen). Pick up the controller and the PlayStation buttons come back by themselves. Keys you bind also follow your keyboard layout (AZERTY, QWERTZ...). Details: [docs/keyboard-prompts.md](https://github.com/Mevlock/uncharted_pc-dev/blob/dev/docs/keyboard-prompts.md).
- **The US version (CUSA02320) is supported.** Its games are built differently from the EU ones, so every fix that needs a place in the game's code now has its US place too: render resolution and ultrawide, starting Uncharted 1 straight from the launcher, the mouse camera, fast mouse aiming (the same turn speeds as EU), the Uncharted 2 crash fixes and the Uncharted 3 heap guard.
- **Light shaft fog renders correctly** (the fix comes from [zorde23's port](https://github.com/zorde23/Uncharted_Collection_PC)): one kind of shader instruction ran for only part of the pixels it should have.
- **Missing one-off effects:** a light or glow drawn for just a frame or two (for example part of Uncharted 3's flashlight) could be missing, because shaders compiled on extra threads arrived too late. Those threads are now off by default.
- **Ultrawide:** aiming close to a wall no longer switches the view to 16:9.
- **Depth of field** setting: it removes Uncharted 3's aiming blur as well as Uncharted 2's; the launcher now says so.

## Known issues
- Uncharted 3: single-frame lighting flashes are rarer since 0.3.6.7 but may not be gone entirely.
- Uncharted 1 and 2 with Quality, Balanced or Performance, and Uncharted 2 at native resolution, work in the opening areas but haven't had a full playthrough (cutscene videos, HUD, menus).
- Ultrawide: short scripted scenes can still switch to 16:9 by mistake; **Always fill** avoids it.
- Rarely, the game freezes while exiting; if it happens, end `bb-probe.exe` in Task Manager.
- Fast mouse aiming covers the EU (CUSA02343) and US (CUSA02320) versions; other versions keep stick-style aiming.
- DLSS Frame Generation goes up to 4×.
