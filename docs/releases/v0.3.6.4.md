**Unofficial fork build.** The official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc); please don't report problems with this build to him. This is SuperMedo's 0.3.6 plus this fork's changes; everything in 0.3.6.3 plus the list below.

**Installing:** unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe`. Your launcher settings carry over, but saves live in each install's own `user\savedata` folder: copy that folder over from your previous install, or set **Saves folder** in the launcher to it.

## New in 0.3.6.4
- **Fixed a crash with DLSS Frame Generation on wide screens** ("the graphics device was lost" as gameplay starts). The first few gameplay frames are still shown at 16:9 while the port switches to the full width, but the scene's depth and motion are already full width; Frame Generation can't take inputs larger than its picture and wrote out of bounds. Those few frames are now skipped and generation starts on the first full-width one. Seen most at 3× and 4×.
- **NVIDIA DLSS updated to 310.9.16** (from 310.9.1): the latest DLSS SDK, with an updated Frame Generation library. The DLSS 4.5 models L and M are available as before (launcher: DLSS model).
- The README explains the Uncharted 3 Sully shirt fix.

## Known issues
- Uncharted 1 and 2 with Quality, Balanced or Performance, and Uncharted 2 at native resolution, work in the opening areas but haven't had a full playthrough (cutscene videos, HUD, menus, frame generation).
- Ultrawide: short scripted scenes can still switch to 16:9 by mistake; **Always fill** avoids it.
- DLSS Frame Generation goes up to 4×; NVIDIA's 5× and 6× modes are not available in this build.
