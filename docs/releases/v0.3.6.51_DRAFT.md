**Unofficial fork build.** The official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc); please don't report problems with this build to him. Everything in 0.3.6.50 plus the changes below.

**Installing:** the launcher of 0.3.6.30 or newer offers this update itself; or unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe`.

## Faster
- **Higher frame rates where the CPU is the limit, and fewer drops in crowded scenes.** The renderer's threads no longer
  share memory they each change all the time (they slowed each other down), the build is tuned for the current code, and
  two kinds of per-draw bookkeeping got cheaper: GPU fences are written more directly, and ranges the GPU already writes are
  no longer re-checked under a lock (most noticeable in crowds, where the lowest frame rates came up the most).

  Measured in Uncharted 3's crowded market, 4 runs each, against 0.3.6.50, on one PC (RTX 4090, 3440×1440):

  | Setup | 0.3.6.50 | 0.3.6.51 | Average | Lowest moments |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Native resolution, DLSS, no frame generation, uncapped | TODO | TODO | TODO | TODO |
  | DLSS Performance, uncapped (CPU-limited) | TODO | TODO | TODO | TODO |
  | Same, with the CPU slowed down (8 threads at 60% speed) | TODO | TODO | TODO | TODO |

  The gain depends on your PC: it's largest when the CPU, not the graphics card, sets your frame rate (slower CPUs, lower
  resolutions, upscaling). If your graphics card is the limit, expect little change. The slowed-down CPU only stands in for a
  weaker processor; a real one may differ.

## Mouse
- **New mouse look in all three games, based on BilalReloaded's approach.** The mouse now turns the camera inside the
  game's own camera update, so every mouse movement counts: a quick flick turns as far as a slow move of the same
  distance (before, fast flicks lost a large part of the turn), and the speed is the same at every frame rate (before, it
  turned about 1.5× faster at 240 FPS than at 60). Aiming, cover and the Uncharted 1 jeep gun use it too, on every game
  version the port supports.
  - **The default speed is lower:** the same sensitivity value now turns about 2.4× less than before. If you had raised
    the sensitivity, check it again.
  - **Up and down on the normal camera** follows the game's own camera arc, as the right stick does; while aiming it
    moves 1:1.
- **Mouse in the menus of all three games** (from antikmewzik's work): the highlight follows the cursor, a click selects,
  the wheel scrolls and the right or side button goes back, in the pause menus too. It is active only while the keyboard
  or mouse is what you used last, so it never fights a controller.

## Button prompts
- **New keyboard, mouse and Xbox button pictures by antikmewzik:** keys drawn as key caps in the game's own font, mouse
  buttons as a mouse, Xbox buttons hand-drawn with coloured halos, also for the prompts in the game world (interact button,
  quick-time prompts, d-pad, journal, sticks), on every game version.
- **Prompts that still showed PlayStation buttons with a keyboard are fixed:** Square, Cross, Circle, L1, L2, R1, R2 and
  Uncharted 3's hold-Triangle (for example on the cargo net in chapter 18).

## New
- **Pause in the background** (launcher **Display**, off by default): while the game's window is minimized or another window
  has the focus, the game stands still, and its pause menu opens when you come back to gameplay (from El Dorado's launcher).
- **The counter names DLAA:** DLSS at your screen's own resolution now reads "DLAA", followed by the DLSS model in use
  ("auto" when the graphics driver chooses).
- **Shader preparation in three clear steps,** with one progress bar running from 0 to 100% on the page and in the launcher:
  the page now also saves the prepared shaders before the game starts (step 3), so the first minutes of play after a fresh
  preparation are no longer slowed down by the saving.
- **The mouse cursor hides** after two seconds without mouse movement while you play with a controller.

## Fixed
- **Intel graphics: no more freeze in Uncharted 3's chateau forest (chapter 6) and other places** where a shadow-filter
  shader made the GPU wait forever. The shader's synchronisation now always runs for every part of the GPU, as on the PS4.
  Tested on an Intel Arc laptop.
- **No more crash when jumping to a checkpoint** from the in-game menu (seen in Uncharted 2): the game's data is briefly
  invalid while a checkpoint loads, and a few of those values stopped the game instead of being ignored (two of the fixes
  from BilalReloaded's port, via El Dorado).
- **Slow drives:** when writing the shader cache is very slow (an antivirus checking every new file, or a hard drive), the
  cache now writes one file at a time, leaving the drive to the game's own loading, and the preparation page doesn't wait
  minutes for it. Excluding the game folder from your antivirus still helps a lot.
- **The launcher no longer shows "Step 2 of 2 ... 60 %" while you play** after a preparation.
