**Unofficial fork build.** The official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc); please don't report problems with this build to him. Everything in 0.3.6.46 plus the change below.

**Installing:** the launcher of 0.3.6.30 or newer offers this update itself; or unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe`.

## Fixed
- **Uncharted 3: falling through the cruise ship after a restart:** in chapters 13 and 14 (*Rough Seas* and *Cruisin' for a Bruisin'*), restarting a checkpoint above about 70 FPS could make the camera jump away, the shipping containers and crates vanish for a moment, and Drake fall through the deck, which killed him and restarted the checkpoint again, over and over. This comes from the original game: when a character steps from one moving surface onto another, the game counted the gap between the two surfaces as movement for that frame. At a restart Drake touches two surfaces in his first frames, and at high frame rates that one-frame "jump" threw the camera about 16 m, which made the level hide the deck cargo Drake was standing on. The frame where a character steps onto another surface now counts as no movement, in every scene. Riding moving vehicles, boats and the cargo plane is unchanged.
