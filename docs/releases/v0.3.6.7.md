**Unofficial fork build.** The official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc); please don't report problems with this build to him. This is SuperMedo's 0.3.6 plus this fork's changes; everything in 0.3.6.6 plus the list below.

**Installing:** unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe`. Your launcher settings carry over, but saves live in each install's own `user\savedata` folder: copy that folder over from your previous install, or set **Saves folder** in the launcher to it.

## New in 0.3.6.7
- **Raw mouse aim in all three games.** Aiming with the mouse used to work like a right stick, capped at about 46 degrees a second however fast you moved, so quick turns felt sluggish. Now the aim follows the mouse 1:1 (0.15 degrees per pixel at sensitivity 1.0, both directions), with no cap and no acceleration. Measured with scripted mouse movement: 300, 1200 and 4800 pixels a second turn 45, 179 and about 790 degrees a second in Uncharted 2 and 3 (before: 10, 35 and 46). Uncharted 1 already turned freely sideways; its up and down aim was under half speed and now matches. Very fast flicks turn about 10% further than they should. `BB_RAW_AIM=0` in Extra variables brings back the old behaviour.
- **Depth of field option** (launcher, Display page, under Bloom): *On* (the game's own), *Off while aiming* or *Off*. Uncharted 2 blurs the whole background while you aim; *Off while aiming* keeps aiming sharp and leaves cutscenes and normal play as they were. Uncharted 2 only so far.
- **Uncharted 3: single-frame lighting flashes fixed** (for example the flashlight's light disappearing for a frame in the mine). They came from the idle job worker change in 0.3.6.4: sleeping workers sometimes woke too late for the frame. Workers now keep spinning for a few milliseconds before they sleep, so they stay awake while the game is busy. In the mine this is also faster: 134 FPS, against 124 with 0.3.6.6.
- Six recent graphics fixes from shadPS4 (5 to 19 October): CE/DE counter synchronisation, texture data kept when a parent image is first created, depth-overlap subresources initialised, pending fast clears applied on FMASK decompress, and two shader translation fixes. Shaders compile again once on the first start.
- Launcher: no longer stops at start with "Could not determine home directory" when started without a home folder in its environment.

## Known issues
- Uncharted 1 and 2 with Quality, Balanced or Performance, and Uncharted 2 at native resolution, work in the opening areas but haven't had a full playthrough (cutscene videos, HUD, menus).
- Ultrawide: short scripted scenes can still switch to 16:9 by mistake; **Always fill** avoids it.
- Rarely, the game froze while exiting after a long session; it hasn't happened again in later 20-minute tests.
- Raw aim covers the EU version (CUSA02343) and has untested locations for CUSA02344; the US version (CUSA02320) keeps the old stick-style aiming.
- DLSS Frame Generation goes up to 4×.
