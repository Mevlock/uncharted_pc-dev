**Private test build: DLSS reworked.** It is 0.3.6.56 plus everything waiting for 0.3.6.57 plus the DLSS changes below; the normal release stays [the latest one](https://github.com/Mevlock/uncharted_pc-dev/releases/latest). Unofficial fork build; the official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc), please don't report problems with this build to him.

**Installing:** unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe` (or over a copy of your install; your saves and settings in `user` are kept).

> ([uncharted_pc_controls_qol](https://github.com/antikmewzik/uncharted_pc_controls_qol)).

## What changes
- **The jitter DLSS needs now comes from the game's own camera.** DLSS wants every frame drawn a fraction of a pixel
  off. Until now the port shifted the scene's draws and then fixed, one by one, the passes that did not follow
  (shadows, decals, single panels) and added workarounds for fades after loads. Now the game's own projection carries
  the shift, so every pass that uses the main camera follows by itself. All three games, versions 1.00 and 1.02, on
  NVIDIA cards; the earlier workarounds are switched off with it.
- **Moving characters carry their own motion.** DLSS only knew how the camera moved, so faces, hair, clothes and held
  weapons could smear while a character moved. Skinned characters now give their real motion.
- **At Native (DLAA) DLSS works before the game's own effects.** It now runs on the scene before depth of field,
  bloom, motion blur and the tone mapping instead of on the finished picture, so those effects are no longer mixed
  into what DLSS accumulates. The sharpening stays on the finished picture (before the HUD), as before.
- **Sharper textures with DLSS**: one more level of texture detail, as NVIDIA recommends.
- **DLSS frame generation gets the real camera** and is told where the HUD is, to keep the HUD out of generated frames.
- Smaller things: the last pixel row and column are tidied before the upscaler (edge shimmer), a history reset when
  the picture jumps more than half its width in one frame, model M at Performance and L at Ultra Performance when the
  DLSS model is left on "default", the treasure model with upscaling.
- **From the coming 0.3.6.57:** faster start of all three games; Uncharted 1's flashlight on ultrawide screens
  (thanks to Bazovsky, PR #33); the cutscene videos on ultrawide screens.

Character motion vectors are taken from BilalReloaded's Uncharted port (GPL-2.0-or-later), as is the treasure fix;
several ideas (texture detail, edge row, cut reset, upscaling before the effects, real camera for frame generation)
come from LanternEngine by Awaken, and the camera jitter idea from the inFAMOUS Second Son port.

## What to check
- Shimmer or crawling on thin detail: fences, railings, wires, grates, hair, distant ground textures.
- Moving characters: faces, hair, clothes, weapons while walking and in cutscenes. Any smear, or flicker at their
  outlines?
- Haze, snow, dust, smoke and fire.
- The first seconds after a load, a checkpoint restart or a cutscene cut: stripes, dotted bands, a "settling" picture?
- Mirrors and water reflections; shadows (sun and torch).
- The HUD, subtitles and button prompts, especially with frame generation on.
- Sharpness compared with 0.3.6.56 at the same Anti-aliasing sharpness setting.
- Standing still: the picture must not shake. A slow camera pan: does anything wobble?

## Known rough edges
- **More shader compiles the first time through each area** (short hitches once): the new character-motion shaders are
  not in the shipped preparation lists yet.
- AMD and Intel cards are untested: there the camera jitter stays off and those cards jitter as in 0.3.6.56 (the other
  changes apply).
- No frame-rate comparison has been made yet. FSR 3.1, FSR 4, XeSS and the scaled modes (Quality to Performance) got
  the shared changes but little testing.

## Turning things off (launcher: Advanced > Environment)
- `BB_PROJECTION_JITTER=0`: the jitter as in 0.3.6.56 (with its workarounds). `BB_PROJECTION_JITTER=1`: tries the
  camera jitter on an AMD or Intel card.
- `BB_CHARACTER_MOTION=0`: camera motion only.
- `BB_NATIVE_BEFORE_POST=0`: DLSS on the finished picture at Native, as before. `BB_SHARPEN_AFTER_POST=0`: sharpening
  before the game's effects.
- `BB_DLSS_MIP_BIAS=0`: the old texture detail (or `-0.5` for half).
- `BB_DLSSG_CAMERA=0`, `BB_DLSSG_UI_ALPHA=0`: frame generation as before.
- With the camera jitter on, the old workarounds can be put back one by one: `BB_DLSS_FADE_RESET=1`,
  `BB_DLSS_FADE_NOJITTER=1`, `BB_MOTION_DEADZONE=0.01`.
- Everything as 0.3.6.56:
  `BB_PROJECTION_JITTER=0 BB_CHARACTER_MOTION=0 BB_NATIVE_BEFORE_POST=0 BB_DLSS_MIP_BIAS=0 BB_UPSCALE_EDGE_CLAMP=0 BB_CUT_MOTION=0 BB_DLSS_AUTO_MODEL=0 BB_UI_DEPTH_ADOPT=0 BB_DLSSG_CAMERA=0`
