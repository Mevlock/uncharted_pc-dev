**Private test build: DLSS reworked.** It is 0.3.6.56 plus everything waiting for 0.3.6.57 plus the DLSS changes below; the normal release stays [the latest one](https://github.com/Mevlock/uncharted_pc-dev/releases/latest). Unofficial fork build; the official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc), please don't report problems with this build to him.

**Installing:** unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe` (or over a copy of your install; your saves and settings in `user` are kept). The launcher shows version 0.3.6.57.1.

## What changed
DLSS wants every frame drawn a fraction of a pixel off (jitter). Until now the port shifted the scene's draws itself
and then fixed, one by one, the passes that did not follow (shadows, decals, single panels), and added workarounds for
the fades after loads and restarts: those were the stripes, dotted bands and "settling" pictures you reported. Now the
game's own camera carries the shift, so every pass that uses the camera follows by itself. **The old workarounds are
switched off on purpose**: this build is to find out whether they are still needed anywhere. All three games, versions
1.00 and 1.02, NVIDIA cards. Also: at Native (DLAA) in Uncharted 2 and 3, DLSS now works on the scene before the
game's depth of field, bloom and motion blur instead of on the finished picture; one more level of texture detail
with DLSS; the last pixel row and column are tidied (edge shimmer); DLSS frame generation gets the real camera.

**Uncharted 3 is darker at high resolutions, and that is the fix.** The game's exposure meter only looked at the left
part of the picture at anything above 1080p, so at 1440p ultrawide or 4K scenes came out up to a third too bright.
It now meters the whole picture, as on a PS4. `BB_EXPOSURE_FIX=0` in the launcher's Extra variables (Advanced >
Environment) restores the old, brighter Uncharted 3 if you want to compare.

- **Uncharted 3, chapter 18, checkpoint #389 (first day's walk in the desert): Pause > Restart checkpoint.** The dotted
  band in the haze and in Drake's dust trail for the first second or so, at 4K render.
- **Uncharted 3, chapter 2, right after a load:** the chain railings while walking; and any "the picture settles for a
  few seconds" after a load, a new chapter or a cutscene (the artifacts after cutscenes at 4K).
- **Uncharted 3, the chateau:** the wall panels.
- **Uncharted 2, chapter 17 (Mountaineering), checkpoint #213, the start of the ice cave:** stripes for 3 to 4 seconds
  after a load or a checkpoint restart.
- **Uncharted 2:** the snow at the start of chapter 2, the dust in chapter 9, the train (chapters 13 and 14).
- **Uncharted 1:** decals (bullet holes, marks on walls and floors) and the sun's shadows: do they shake or crawl?
- Everywhere: standing still, the picture must not shake; in a slow pan nothing should wobble; fences, railings, wires
  and hair should not crawl more than in 0.3.6.56.

The checkpoint numbers are the ones on the overlay's Checkpoints page (Insert).

## If something is still wrong
Please send `user\last_run.log` from that session and a screenshot or a short clip, and say which game, chapter or
checkpoint, your Anti-aliasing and Render resolution settings and the DLSS model. If you can, try the same spot once
more with the old workarounds back (the same Extra variables box) and say whether it changes:
`BB_DLSS_FADE_RESET=1 BB_DLSS_FADE_NOJITTER=1 BB_MOTION_DEADZONE=0.01`, or everything as 0.3.6.56 with
`BB_PROJECTION_JITTER=0 BB_EXPOSURE_FIX=0 BB_NATIVE_BEFORE_POST=0 BB_DLSS_MIP_BIAS=0 BB_UPSCALE_EDGE_CLAMP=0 BB_CUT_MOTION=0 BB_DLSS_AUTO_MODEL=0 BB_UI_DEPTH_ADOPT=0 BB_DLSSG_CAMERA=0`.

## Known gaps
- Characters' own motion for DLSS is **off** in this build (it is not finished); characters move with the camera's
  motion as in 0.3.6.56.
- AMD and Intel cards keep the old jitter; only the other changes apply there. Untested.
- Quality, Balanced and Performance got little testing; no need to test them. Experimental, also not something you
  need to test: `BB_SCALED_BEFORE_POST=1` with a Render resolution below Native lets DLSS work before the game's
  effects in those modes too; the FPS counter then shows e.g. `DLSS K 1712x704 P pre`.
- From the coming 0.3.6.57, also in here: faster start of all three games, Uncharted 1's flashlight on ultrawide
  screens (thanks to Bazovsky, PR #33), the cutscene videos on ultrawide screens.

The treasure-model fix and the (switched off) character motion are taken from BilalReloaded's Uncharted port
(GPL-2.0-or-later); several ideas (texture detail, edge row, cut reset, upscaling before the effects, real camera for
frame generation) come from LanternEngine by Awaken, and the camera jitter idea from the inFAMOUS Second Son port.
