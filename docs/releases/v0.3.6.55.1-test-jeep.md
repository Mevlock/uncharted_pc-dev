**Test build: Uncharted 1 judder on PCs with 8 CPU threads or fewer** (for example the chapter 7 jeep ride). It is 0.3.6.55 plus one change; the normal release stays [the latest one](https://github.com/Mevlock/uncharted_pc-dev/releases/latest). Unofficial fork build; the official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc).

**Installing:** unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe` (or over a copy of your install; your saves and settings in `user` are kept).

> **A big thank you to [antikmewzik](https://github.com/antikmewzik)** for his keyboard, mouse and controller work
> ([uncharted_pc_controls_qol](https://github.com/antikmewzik/uncharted_pc_controls_qol)).

## What changes
- **Smoother motion on CPUs with 8 threads or fewer.** On those PCs the port lets the game's idle worker threads sleep
  instead of spinning, to leave the CPU to the game (spinning would keep about 6 cores busy and cost frame rate on a
  small CPU). A sleeping worker could take up to 10 ms to notice new work, so frames started unevenly. Uncharted 1
  times every frame with the real clock, so the world moved in uneven steps (6 to 23 ms instead of a steady 16.7) even
  at a steady 60 FPS: the jeep and the scenery juddered. Sleeping workers now check every 1 ms.
- Measured at the start of chapter 7 with the workers sleeping: the frame step spread drops from 5 ms to about 1 ms
  (frames more than 2 ms off: 80% before, 8% now), with no extra CPU use (1.8 cores either way).
- PCs with more than 8 threads are not affected (their workers never sleep).
- All three games use the same workers, so Uncharted 2 and 3 can be smoother on these PCs too.

## What to check
- Uncharted 1 chapter 7 (jeep), or any steady camera pan: is the judder gone?
- To compare with the old behaviour, add `BB_JOB_SLEEP_MS=10` in the launcher (Advanced > Environment).
- Please send `user\last_run.log`. With `BB_U1_FRAME_STATS=1` added, it shows the frame step spread every 5 seconds.
