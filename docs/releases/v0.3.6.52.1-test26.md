**Test build for [issue #26](https://github.com/Mevlock/uncharted_pc-dev/issues/26)** (Mailbox judder on a 60 Hz screen without VRR). It is 0.3.6.52.1 plus one change; the normal release stays [the latest one](https://github.com/Mevlock/uncharted_pc-dev/releases/latest). Unofficial fork build; the official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc).

**Installing:** unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe` (or over a copy of your install; your saves and settings in `user` are kept).

> **A big thank you to [antikmewzik](https://github.com/antikmewzik)** for his keyboard, mouse and controller work
> ([uncharted_pc_controls_qol](https://github.com/antikmewzik/uncharted_pc_controls_qol)).

## What changes
- **The game's 60 Hz clock now follows your screen's real refresh.** Before, the port ran it on its own timer at exactly
  60.000 times a second. Most "60 Hz" screens run at 59.94 or 59.97 Hz, so with **Mailbox** the two clocks slowly slid
  past each other: every so often frames were dropped and shown twice for a while (a judder at a steady 60 FPS), then it
  went away and came back. Now the port measures the screen's refresh and keeps its clock in step with it.
- Only when the screen refreshes at nearly the game's rate (60 FPS on a 60 Hz screen, and so on). A faster screen
  (120/144 Hz with the game at 60) keeps the timer as before.
- The log (`user\last_run.log`) says what it found, for example
  `VideoOut: display refresh measured 59.970 Hz, vblank 60.000 Hz: the vblank follows the display's refresh`.

Measured on a monitor set to 59.97 Hz, Mailbox, 60 FPS: the judder about every 30 seconds is gone, with no extra
uneven frames (0.1 frames over 25 ms per 5 seconds, as before).
