## Saves, as on the console
- **Your games.** Each campaign save is a game of its own, as on the PS4. *Save game* (pause menu), *Chapter Select*
  and *New Game* list your games and **New save**; *Load game* lists your games. The game you are playing is marked and
  listed first.
- **Undo.** Before the game saves over one of your games (Chapter Select or New Game over a game, or *Save game* onto
  another game than the one you play), a copy is kept. *Load game* offers it as **Undo**; loading it puts that game back
  as it was, and the save it replaces becomes the next Undo.
- **Delete** in the lists (Triangle / Y twice): a game moves to the `savedata-deleted` folder, it is not erased. The game
  you are playing can't be deleted.
- **Save pictures.** Each game shows a picture of where you were, taken from the scene without the HUD or the menus. A
  new game shows the game's icon until you first save it yourself from the pause menu; after that its picture follows
  your play. Chapter Select or New Game over a game starts it afresh (its old picture stays with its Undo copy).
- **A new look for the save list**: cards with the picture, the game's own progress text and the date, sized to the
  list at every resolution and on ultrawide screens.
- **The in-game menu's Saves page** (Insert) shows the same: your games and Undo, with Load (the game restarts and
  *Continue* starts there) and Delete. The old save slots are gone from it; their folders stay on disk.
- **Keep this progress** (practice mode, after a checkpoint jump) now makes the practice save a new game of its own; your
  other games stay as they are.
- Practice mode shows the same lists (it saves to its practice copies).

## Cheats
- **No Reload, One-Hit Kills and Infinite Grenades** for all three games, next to Infinite Health and Infinite Ammo
  (launcher, Cheats). One-Hit Kills affects only your attacks; allies and enemies hit as before.

## DLSS
- **No shimmer or dither pattern during fades.** While the picture fades in or out (loads, checkpoint restarts, the
  white haze of Uncharted 3's desert), DLSS no longer moves its sample position, which showed the game's dithered haze
  and dust as a blocky pattern for a second or two.
- **No settling shimmer after a load.** Fine detail such as chains and railings no longer shimmers for a few seconds
  when you start walking right after a load (Uncharted 1 and 3). Uncharted 2 keeps the reset that stops the striped
  snow in its ice cave.
- **Still a work in progress:** some DLSS artifacts remain in places, and the work on them goes on.

## Fixes
- **Quit on every pause page** in Uncharted 2 and 3 (it was missing on some of them until the usual page was shown).
- **The mouse works on the Explorer warning** in Chapter Select ("Player stats, trophies ... are disabled in this mode.
  Continue?") in all three games.
- **Uncharted 1's pause menu Save Game** is recognised, so its saves get the *Save game* list and their pictures.
- **Missing letters or a black picture in the in-game menu** (rarely, for a whole session): its text and pictures could
  fail to reach the GPU when they were uploaded at the same moment as the game's own work.
- The new texts are translated into all six languages of the in-game menu.
