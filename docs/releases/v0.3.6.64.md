## Fix
- **AMD graphics cards: some of the port's game fixes could be skipped at start-up.** On AMD cards the graphics driver
  took the memory those fixes need, so up to a handful of them were silently left out each start. They now have room
  reserved before the graphics card starts, so every fix is installed on all cards.

## DLSS and FSR picture
- **Cleaner motion for moving objects and the camera** (from 0.3.6.59): the upscalers get each moving object's motion
  from all of its draws and the camera's motion from the frame before, so fast-moving things smear and ghost less.
  The object motion also costs about a third less CPU time than before.
- **Riders get their own motion** (from 0.3.6.59): characters riding vehicles, trains, horses and moving platforms
  now get their own motion instead of the world's passing by, so they smear less.
