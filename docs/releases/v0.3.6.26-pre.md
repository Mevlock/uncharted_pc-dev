**Pre-release: needs testing.** Crash fixes and the Uncharted 1 mouse aiming. Tested in all three games on the EU and US versions, in scripted runs and play sessions.

**Unofficial fork build.** The official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc); please don't report problems with this build to him. This is SuperMedo's 0.3.6 plus this fork's changes; everything in 0.3.6.16 plus the list below.

**Installing:** unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe`. Your launcher settings carry over, but saves live in each install's own `user\savedata` folder: copy that folder over from your previous install, or set **Saves folder** in the launcher to it.

## New in 0.3.6.26 (pre-release)
- **Fixed random crashes at high frame rates** (all three games, since 0.3.6.21; mostly after a few minutes above about 120 FPS). The fix for the Uncharted 2 freezes in 0.3.6.21 moved some frame flips to a thread of their own, but preparing a frame uses drawing state only the GPU command thread may use, so the two threads sometimes worked on it at the same time: the graphics device was lost ("write to an invalid address at 0x0") or the game crashed. Those flips now go to the GPU command thread, like the game's other flips; the freeze stays fixed. Reported by ngcvenusseadee-cyber.
- **Fixed a crash at the title screen with frame generation on ultrawide screens** (DLSS Frame Generation with the Balanced render resolution, all three games). The title and menu pictures are 16:9 while the 3D scene behind them is already drawn for the wide screen; frame generation was set up for the 16:9 picture with scene data of another shape and lost the graphics device. Such frames are now shown without generated frames in between, and generation starts once the game's own picture fills the screen.
- **Uncharted 2 and 3: aiming no longer blurs the whole view at render sizes other than 1920 × 1080** (native resolution on wide or high-resolution screens, native 4K, the upscaled modes). The game measures the distance under the crosshair to set its depth of field, but worked out the crosshair's position for a 1920 × 1080 picture: at 3840 × 2160 it measured a quarter of the way in from the top-left corner, often something right next to the camera, and blurred everything behind Drake. It now measures under the crosshair at every render size (in the Uncharted 2 museum the focus went from about 1 m back to 11.7 m, as at 1080p). If you set **Depth of field** to "Off while aiming" because of this, "On" now looks as intended. EU, US and EU/Russian versions. Reported by adelhmv-glitch.
- **Uncharted 1: the mouse aim no longer stands still for up to a second when you start aiming.** Uncharted 1 blends from the normal camera into the aiming camera, and the 1:1 aiming from 0.3.6.25 only turned the aiming camera, which barely shows at first (and sometimes ignores turns until the game switches cameras). For the first 0.4 seconds of aiming the mouse now turns the view the way a controller's stick does, then 1:1 as before. In a scripted test of 30 aims the view started turning after 110 ms instead of 250 ms (at worst 0.5 instead of 1 second). `BB_AIM_STICK_MS=<ms>` in **Environment** changes the 0.4 seconds; `0` turns it off. Reported by ngcvenusseadee-cyber.

## From 0.3.6.25 (pre-release)
- **New mouse aiming in all three games: true 1:1.** The mouse now turns the game's own aim direction instead of acting as a fast right stick, so aiming turns exactly as far as looking around does, at any frame rate, with the game's aim assist and recoil still working. Rolling straight into aim points where you look, fast mouse flicks no longer swing the camera up over Drake's head, and the mouse now also works in cover. EU, US and EU/Russian versions. By Umar-Qayyum-dev (ported to the EU version here).
- **Uncharted 3: fixed round red and blue spots in the bloom** (soft blotches floating over the picture, mostly in darker areas, in many chapters). Reported by adelhmv-glitch; fix after zorde23's.
- **Native 4K: cutscene videos no longer show zoomed in** (3840 × 2160 render resolution, all three games). Reported by adelhmv-glitch.
- **Ultrawide: mirrors and water look right at the sides of the screen.**
- **Unlock all chapters** (launcher, Console page). By BilalReloaded.
- **US and EU/Russian versions: the 4-core crash fix from 0.3.6.23 now applies to them too.**
- **Smoother camera turning in all three games** (mouse and controller): on CPUs with more than 8 threads the game's job threads keep running between jobs, as on the PS4. **Display → Idle game threads** chooses: Automatic, Spin or Sleep.
- **Sleeping job threads no longer miss work** (Uncharted 3's one-frame lighting flashes).
- **EU version (CUSA02343): idle job threads now hand the CPU to other work.**
- Shader translation and texture-cache fixes from shadPS4. The shader cache is rebuilt once on the first start of each game.
- **The launcher keeps the last five run logs.**

Older changes: see the 0.3.6.25 release notes.
