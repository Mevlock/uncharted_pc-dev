**Test build: faster handling of GPU memory the game rewrites** (big frame drops in heavy scenes such as Uncharted 3's
horse chase, most of all on slower CPUs). It is 0.3.6.52.1 plus one change; the normal release stays
[the latest one](https://github.com/Mevlock/uncharted_pc-dev/releases/latest). Unofficial fork build; the official port is
[SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc).

**Installing:** unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe` (or over a copy of your install; your saves and settings
in `user` are kept).

> **A big thank you to [antikmewzik](https://github.com/antikmewzik)** for his keyboard, mouse and controller work
> ([uncharted_pc_controls_qol](https://github.com/antikmewzik/uncharted_pc_controls_qol)).

## What changes
- **Less waiting when the game writes to memory the GPU used.** In scenes with many animated characters (Uncharted 3's
  Caravan horse chase, chapter 20) the game copies its characters' bone data into memory the GPU wrote a few frames
  earlier, thousands of times a second. Each time, the game thread waited for the port to fetch the GPU's data back
  (about 0.1 ms). The port now copies that data back ahead of time, at the end of the GPU's work, so the game finds it
  ready (about 0.02 ms).

Measured in the horse chase (2-minute ride from the checkpoint after the opening cutscene, 3440x1440, RTX 5080, four runs
each with and without the change):
- a fast CPU: about **+4%** average frame rate;
- the same PC limited to 8 threads at 60% speed (a stand-in for a slower CPU): about **+20%** average and **+14%** in the
  worst 20 seconds (99 to 118 FPS, worst stretch 81 to 93).

Scenes without this kind of work hardly change (Uncharted 3's market: +0.5 to +1.4%). Your numbers will differ with
your CPU, GPU and settings. The simulated slow CPU is not a real older processor, so reports from players with one are
the most useful.

## Please tell us
- Your CPU and GPU, and whether heavy scenes (the horse chase, big fights, crowds) run smoother or the same.
- Anything that looks wrong that did not before: flickering or stretched character models, crashes. If so, set
  `BB_PREFETCH_READBACK=0` in the launcher (Advanced > Environment) to turn the change off, and tell us whether that fixes it.
- Your `user\last_run.log` if something goes wrong.
