## New cheats (launcher > Cheats)
All three games now have the same set: **Infinite Health, Infinite Ammo, No Reload, One-Hit Kills, Infinite Grenades**
and **Developer mode**. Each works on the EU and the US/Russian/Spanish releases.

- **One-Hit Kills** in Uncharted 1 and 2 (Uncharted 3 had it already): every hit of yours kills an enemy at once.
  Only your attacks count: allies (Elena, Chloe, Sully...) and enemies hit as usual, so an ally is never killed by one
  enemy shot.
- **Infinite Grenades** in all three games: throwing a grenade no longer uses one up. Enemies keep theirs.
- **No Reload** in Uncharted 1 too. In Uncharted 1 the clip is your ammo, so No Reload and Infinite Ammo do the same
  there.

## Also
- 15 more shader variants prepared ahead (from a player's cache): fewer first-time stutters in a few places.

## What to check
- With **One-Hit Kills** on: one body shot kills an enemy; your ally still needs several hits to bring one down, and
  enemies don't kill your ally in one shot.
- With **Infinite Grenades** on: the grenade count stays the same after throws, both the quick throw and the aimed one
  (in Uncharted 2 especially).
- Please report problems with `user\last_run.log` attached.
