**Pre-release: needs testing.** A crash fix for Uncharted 2 at high frame rates. Tested on the EU and US versions.

**Unofficial fork build.** The official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc); please don't report problems with this build to him. This is SuperMedo's 0.3.6 plus this fork's changes; everything in 0.3.6.27 plus the list below.

**Installing:** unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe`. Your launcher settings carry over, but saves live in each install's own `user\savedata` folder: copy that folder over from your previous install, or set **Saves folder** in the launcher to it.

## New in 0.3.6.28 (pre-release)
- **Uncharted 2: no more stop on the game's own check `pAction != NULL`** (`moveactioncontroller.cpp`), seen in fights, often right after a jump or a takedown. The game keeps Drake's current moves in a list with room for 64. When two moves take turns every frame, as when landing straight into a fight, the game does not let go of one move each time they swap. The original runs at 30 frames a second; at 144 the same moment fills the list several times faster, and a long enough one filled it and stopped the game. The list now has room for 256 (about 150 KB more memory). In tests at 144 FPS the list held up to 30 at the busiest moments. EU, US and EU/Russian versions.

## From 0.3.6.27 (pre-release)
- **Graphics cards with 4 to 6 GB of video memory: no more crashes when the game runs out of video memory.**
- **New render resolution "Automatic":** native on cards with more than 6 GB, 1920 × 1080 on cards with 6 GB or less.
- **Older Intel processors (up to about the 8th generation):** idle job threads no longer slow the game down when Windows' Meltdown protection is on.
- **Uncharted 3: no more rows of bright blue dots along some walls** with DLSS, FSR or TAA.
- **The FPS counter shows memory use** (RAM and video memory).
- **Uncharted 3: the crash climbing the fort in chapter 8** now resets the look direction instead of stopping the game.
- Crash fixes by drakolordx7 and from the official port's 0.3.9 (BilalReloaded's fixes).

Older changes: see the 0.3.6.27 release notes.
