**Unofficial fork build.** The official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc); please don't report problems with this build to him. Everything in 0.3.6.66 plus the changes below.

**Installing:** the launcher of 0.3.6.30 or newer offers this update itself; or unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe`.

## Changes
- **Overlay > Checkpoints with a controller:** pressing the confirm button (A / Cross) twice quickly on a checkpoint
  jumps there and closes the menu, as a double click does; entering the list with the pad focuses its first
  checkpoint. The hint under the list says so in every language.

## Fixes
- **A rare freeze, most often in a cutscene.** On some PCs the game could stop for good every few hours, often while a
  cutscene played. The game's job system reused a finished job's counter while another job could still change it;
  counters are now handed back only once no job can still touch them. No measurable effect on frame rate (U3 market route, slow and fast CPU, against 0.3.6.66).
- **Uncharted (U1), checkpoint jumps:** jumping from chapter 1 to a checkpoint in chapter 2 could leave the river
  without water (no splashes, fish in the air), also after restarting the checkpoint. The water a jump keeps loaded is
  now switched back on as on a normal load.
- **Uncharted 3: one shader combination was built with its resources in the wrong places.** Shader preparation threw
  it away at every start and the game built it again while playing. It is now built correctly.
- **Textures after level loads:** a draw could be given a texture that had just been replaced (a wrong texture for a
  moment, mostly while a level loads). Not seen on screen in tests, but now ruled out. Same fix as in sourama's
  SpartanPort, as ported to gow3_pc by Mahdi Akbari Zarkesh.
- **Full-screen effects that use stencil** are no longer skipped when their back-face settings differ from the front:
  these passes have no back face on the PS4, and now always use the front settings. Same fix as ItalianKuze's in
  port-ga-gotoku.
- **Stencil buffers** get their full size, including layers, multisampling and padding (they could be smaller than the
  area drawn). Following shadPS4 PR 5296, as taken in port-ga-gotoku.
- **A small memory overwrite at start-up is gone:** the game writes into its locks a little past where the port's lock
  ended. Locks now have the PS4's full size. Same fix as MoonFuji's in driveclub-recomp.
- **Graphics cards that allow a fixed shader width only on some shader stages:** the port now asks for it only on those
  stages, as the Vulkan rules require. The start-up line in `last_run.log` lists the stages.

## For troubleshooting
- New log lines and switches that help find rare bugs; they change nothing in play unless turned on.
