**Unofficial fork build.** The official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc); please don't report problems with this build to him. This is SuperMedo's 0.3.6 plus this fork's changes; everything in 0.3.6.7 plus the list below.

**Installing:** unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe`. Your launcher settings carry over, but saves live in each install's own `user\savedata` folder: copy that folder over from your previous install, or set **Saves folder** in the launcher to it.

## New in 0.3.6.8
- **Faster again: the six shadPS4 graphics fixes from 0.3.6.7 are removed.** They cost about 9% in Uncharted 3 (Cartagena, standing: about 88 FPS with them, 97 without) and fixed nothing we had seen go wrong in these games. Which of them costs is still to be found; the free ones may come back later. Shaders compile again once on the first start.
- Everything else from 0.3.6.7 stays: raw mouse aim in all three games, the Depth of field option and the fix for Uncharted 3's single-frame lighting flashes.

Measured on the same save and spot, alternating builds to cancel out the PC's own drift: this build is close to 0.3.6.2 (within about 3%). The remaining difference comes from the flash fix keeping the game's job workers awake a little longer, which helps in some scenes (the mine, +8%) and costs a little in busy ones.

## Known issues
- Uncharted 1 and 2 with Quality, Balanced or Performance, and Uncharted 2 at native resolution, work in the opening areas but haven't had a full playthrough (cutscene videos, HUD, menus).
- Ultrawide: short scripted scenes can still switch to 16:9 by mistake; **Always fill** avoids it.
- Rarely, the game freezes while exiting (seen once more in testing, also in 0.3.6.2); if it happens, end `bb-probe.exe` in Task Manager.
- Raw aim covers the EU version (CUSA02343) and has untested locations for CUSA02344; the US version (CUSA02320) keeps the old stick-style aiming.
- DLSS Frame Generation goes up to 4×.
