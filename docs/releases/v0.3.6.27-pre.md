**Pre-release: needs testing.** Graphics cards with 4 to 6 GB of video memory, crash fixes, older Intel processors and a fix for blue dots on walls in Uncharted 3. Tested in all three games on the EU and US versions, in scripted runs.

**Unofficial fork build.** The official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc); please don't report problems with this build to him. This is SuperMedo's 0.3.6 plus this fork's changes; everything in 0.3.6.26 plus the list below.

**Installing:** unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe`. Your launcher settings carry over, but saves live in each install's own `user\savedata` folder: copy that folder over from your previous install, or set **Saves folder** in the launcher to it.

## New in 0.3.6.27 (pre-release)
- **Graphics cards with 4 to 6 GB of video memory: no more crashes when the game runs out of video memory.** The port now keeps within the card's budget: it lets go of pictures that are not in use, hands back buffer memory that has sat unused for a while (also on bigger cards, where video memory use no longer keeps growing during long sessions), and when something still does not fit it is placed outside the budget instead of stopping the game. On a simulated 4 GB card (about 50 runs at 1920 × 1080, 2560 × 1440 and 3440 × 1440) the game never crashed. Video memory hand-back after BilalReloaded's port.
- **New render resolution "Automatic":** native on cards with more than 6 GB, the game's own 1920 × 1080 on cards with 6 GB or less, where the game fits in video memory. Above 1920 × 1080 a 4 GB card does not have enough memory: the game runs, but can drop to about half speed or stutter. The launcher now says so when your card has 6 GB or less and the render resolution is above 1920 × 1080. FSR needs about 350 MB less video memory than DLSS. Your current choice stays as it is.
- **Older Intel processors (up to about the 8th generation, e.g. i7-7700K):** the game's idle job threads no longer hand their processor core back to Windows thousands of times a second when Windows' Meltdown protection is on, which makes each of those calls slow. A player's i7-7700K went from about 50 to 55 FPS in Uncharted 3 with this change. Other processors keep it as before. `BB_JOB_YIELD=1` or `0` in **Environment** overrides it.
- **Uncharted 3: no more rows of bright blue dots along some walls** (seen in the chateau, chapter 8) with DLSS, FSR or TAA. Some wall panels are drawn as a single flat quad, which the port did not shift by the upscaler's tiny per-frame offset like the rest of the scene; the seams between them and their neighbours opened into pinholes showing the background colour. They now move with the rest of the scene. Native 1920 × 1080 without anti-aliasing never showed it.
- **The FPS counter shows memory use:** a third line with the game's RAM and video memory against your PC's and graphics card's totals, for example `RAM 3.5/32 GB  VRAM 2.8/16 GB`.
- **Uncharted 3: the crash climbing the fort in chapter 8** (the game's `m_lookAt` check) now resets the look direction instead of stopping the game. EU, US and EU/Russian versions. After BilalReloaded's fix in the official port's 0.3.9.
- **Crash fixes by drakolordx7:** a graphics pipeline the driver refuses with the saved shader cache is created again without it; a shader detail (thread-id addressing outside compute) and an unknown texture border colour no longer stop the game.
- **From the official port's 0.3.9** (BilalReloaded's fixes): the game's memory is reserved in full when it is allocated (error 1450 on PCs close to their memory limit), and unusual texture formats and FMask stores no longer stop the game.
