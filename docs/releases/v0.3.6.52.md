**Unofficial fork build.** The official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc); please don't report problems with this build to him. Everything in 0.3.6.51 plus the changes below.

**Installing:** the launcher of 0.3.6.30 or newer offers this update itself; or unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe`.

## Load game shows your saves
- **The games' Load Game now opens a real save list** in all three games, like on the PS4: each save with a picture of
  where you were, the game's progress text and when it was saved. Choose with the controller, the keyboard or the mouse.
  The list and its pictures are ported from BilalReloaded's port (by the-best-is-best).
- **Your save slots are in it too.** Copies you made with **Save here** (press **Insert**, or L3 + R3 on a controller,
  then **Save slots**) show under *Your save slots*. Pick one and the game loads it straight away, with no restart.
- **Undo last load** at the bottom takes you back to where you were before you last loaded something, so a wrong pick
  is never a problem.
- **Delete** (Delete key or Triangle / Y, pressed twice) removes a slot from the list. Nothing is thrown away: it goes
  to a `deleted` folder beside your slots.
- Saving into a slot stays on the overlay's **Save slots** page (the games only save automatically at checkpoints).

## Developer mode
- **Developer mode without the green text.** Cheats > Developer mode has a new option, **Show the debug text**. Untick
  it and the game starts without Naughty Dog's green FPS, timing and memory text in the corner, while the F11 Dev Menu
  and F8 Quick Menu keep working. **F7** shows or hides that text at any time while you play.
- While the green text is hidden, the port's FPS counter shows **DEV MODE** in green, so you can still see it is on.
- **Uncharted 1's Quick Menu (F8)** opens now too: Invincible Player, Give Player Weapon, Targeting and Camera Flip,
  Super Pause and more. Before, only its Dev Menu (F11) opened.

## Fixes
- **FSR 4 on AMD: thin white lines along the right and bottom edges of the picture are gone** (seen on Radeon RX 9070
  XT, at 1080p and 4K). AMD's drivers left out the picture's last column and row on about half the frames, and the
  upscaler smeared that into lines. The camera's tiny upscaler offset now moves the scene itself instead of the edge of
  the picture (the fix follows eFMann's shadPS4 Bloodborne fork, which had the same bug on Radeon RX 9000 cards).
  **AMD and Intel graphics cards prepare their shaders once more after this update** (the preparation page at the first
  start); NVIDIA cards are not affected and keep their prepared shaders.
- The in-game menu's mouse look help and *Camera up and down* are translated now.

## Speed
- **The same as 0.3.6.51.** Measured in Uncharted 3's crowded market, 4 runs each, on one PC (RTX 5080, 3440×1440):
  193.2 against 193.4 FPS with DLSS Performance uncapped (CPU-limited), and 121.4 against 122.5 FPS with the CPU slowed
  down (8 threads at 60% speed). Both are within the few percent results vary between runs.
