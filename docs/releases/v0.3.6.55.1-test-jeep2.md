**Test build: judder on PCs with 8 CPU threads or fewer** (for example the Uncharted 1 chapter 7 jeep ride). It is 0.3.6.55 plus the fix below; the normal release stays [the latest one](https://github.com/Mevlock/uncharted_pc-dev/releases/latest). Unofficial fork build; the official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc).

**Installing:** unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe` (or over a copy of your install; your saves and settings in `user` are kept).

> **A big thank you to [antikmewzik](https://github.com/antikmewzik)** for his keyboard, mouse and controller work
> ([uncharted_pc_controls_qol](https://github.com/antikmewzik/uncharted_pc_controls_qol)).

## What changes
- **Smoother motion on CPUs with 8 threads or fewer.** On those PCs the port lets the game's idle worker threads sleep
  to leave the CPU to the game (keeping them spinning would take about 6 cores and cost frame rate on a small CPU).
  But when a job that was waiting for other work became ready again, the game never woke a sleeping worker: on the
  PS4 its workers never sleep. The job then waited until a worker's sleep ran out (up to 10 ms), and so did the frame.
  Uncharted 1 moves the world by the real time between frames, so the world moved in uneven steps (6 to 23 ms
  instead of a steady 16.7) at a steady 60 FPS: the jeep and the scenery juddered.
- Now the workers are woken as soon as such a job is ready, in all three games (EU and US, versions 1.00 and 1.02).
  Sleeping workers also check for work every 1 ms instead of 10 ms.
- Measured at the start of Uncharted 1 chapter 7 with sleeping workers: frames more than 2 ms off went from 80% to
  about 1.5%, as smooth as spinning workers, with no extra CPU use (about 1.9 cores).
- PCs with more than 8 threads are not affected (their workers never sleep).

## What to check
- Uncharted 1 chapter 7 (jeep), or any steady camera pan in any of the three games: is the judder gone?
- To compare with the old behaviour, add `BB_JOB_RESUME_WAKE=0 BB_JOB_SLEEP_MS=10` in the launcher (Advanced >
  Environment).
- Please send `user\last_run.log`. With `BB_U1_FRAME_STATS=1` added, it shows Uncharted 1's frame step spread every
  5 seconds ("sd" about 1 ms or less with the fix, about 5 without).
