**Unofficial fork build.** The official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc); please don't report problems with this build to him. This is SuperMedo's 0.3.6 plus this fork's changes; everything in 0.3.6.14 plus the list below.

**Installing:** unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe`. Your launcher settings carry over, but saves live in each install's own `user\savedata` folder: copy that folder over from your previous install, or set **Saves folder** in the launcher to it.

## New in 0.3.6.15
- **Uncharted 3: the torch Drake carries lights a round area again** (chapter 9, "The Middle Way", and other hand-held torches) at a **Render resolution above 1920 × 1080** or with **Ultrawide**. It lit a hard-edged square or trapezoid instead. This came from this fork's own resizing, not from the game or the official port, which draws at 1920 × 1080: to show the wider or larger picture, the fork scales the game's camera, and it scaled the torch's shadow cameras as well, so the shadow was drawn with one projection and read with another. Square cameras like those now keep the game's own projection; the view, field of view and ultrawide picture are unchanged.
- **Shadows of the other point lights above 1080p:** at those sizes the game keeps more torch shadows in a larger array, and this port lost the game's clear of the part added later, so a light using it showed full shadow. The clear is now kept. Like the torch, this only showed at the fork's higher render resolutions.
- **Closing the game:** a thread that copies GPU results back for the game could keep the game from exiting; it now gives up on shutdown (by drakolordx7). The rare freeze on exit may have other causes too.
- **Uncharted 1 on GeForce GTX 10 series (Pascal) cards:** the crash with "the graphics device was lost" in the particle effects has an opt-in fix: add `BB_PASCAL_CTA_SPLIT=1` to **Environment** on the launcher's **Advanced** page (by dp6pfpu). Other cards don't need it.
- **Setup accepts decrypted game dumps made with Itemzflow** (plain ELF executables), which stopped at "expected PS4 SELF" (by currantsforeyes, reported by marks-jhojan).
- Two small GPU command fixes (by iannitram).

## Known issues
- Native 4K (the two experimental Render resolution choices) has had only short tests; if a game crashes or looks wrong with one, go back to Native.
- Uncharted 3: single-frame lighting flashes are rarer since 0.3.6.7 but may not be gone entirely.
- Uncharted 1 and 2 with Quality, Balanced or Performance, and Uncharted 2 at native resolution, work in the opening areas but haven't had a full playthrough (cutscene videos, HUD, menus).
- Ultrawide: short scripted scenes can still switch to 16:9 by mistake; **Always fill** avoids it.
- Rarely, the game freezes while exiting; if it happens, end `bb-probe.exe` in Task Manager.
- Fast mouse aiming covers the EU (CUSA02343), US (CUSA02320) and EU/Russian (CUSA02344) versions; other versions keep stick-style aiming.
- DLSS Frame Generation goes up to 4×.
