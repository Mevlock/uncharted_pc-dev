**Unofficial fork build.** The official port is [SuperMedo's uncharted_pc](https://github.com/Supermedo/uncharted_pc); please don't report problems with this build to him. Everything in 0.3.6.36 plus the changes below.

**Installing:** the launcher of 0.3.6.30 or newer offers this update itself; or unpack into a new folder and start `Uncharted.exe`.

## Fixed
- **Flashing surfaces (Uncharted 2 chapter 1 train, and other rare one-frame flashes):** part of the train wreck flashed white for single frames, a few times a minute. The port prepares draws ahead of the work before them, and could get too far ahead of the small copy shaders that fill in material values. It now waits for them when it gets too far ahead. This is free in our frame rate tests. Two smaller gaps of the same kind are closed too. The same causes could make other rare single-frame flashes in all three games.
- **Uncharted 2 SSAO stripes** ("snow bars" on the convoy road and elsewhere): the ambient occlusion now samples depth and normals smoothly, and 64-bit volume textures use the games' own tile order.
- **AMD graphics cards:** flickering menus and black blocks with FSR 1 sharpening at the window's size, and a thin bright line at the right and bottom edges of the picture.
- **Controller:** quick taps between two reads of the pad now count (for example the lever in Uncharted 1's Treasure Vault). The controller keeps working when the game window loses the keyboard focus, and is ignored while another program is in front.
- **Crashes and hangs:**
  - An effect shader in Uncharted 3 (and its relatives in all three games) could loop on a bad count until the graphics driver stopped; the loop is now capped.
  - Bad texture data in a few places could stop the game while a warning was being written to the log.
  - Missing or invalid textures are bound in a form the graphics driver accepts, and sampler detail ranges are always valid.
  - A crash in the texture cache when it grew (from upstream PR #742 by marks-jhojan).
  - Several rare races when the game maps or frees memory while the GPU still uses it.
  - Quitting, switching games from the collection menu and stops on an error now close cleanly instead of hanging.
- **Incomplete game folders:** a game folder without Uncharted 2's or 3's executable now gets a clear message instead of starting Uncharted 1.
- **Textures:** images written by the GPU next to other images refresh correctly (from shadPS4 #5599); textures that declare more detail levels than their size allows are capped (shadPS4 #5453).

## Faster
- **Fewer shader compiles while playing:** more shader variants and pipelines in all three games' lists, prepared at start (from a player's caches after finishing Uncharted 3, and new play sessions).
- The work that prepares draws is spread over the CPU better (a quarter of the threads, at most 4), and several per-draw costs are lower.
- Measured on the developer's PC (Ryzen with 16 threads, RTX 5080) in Uncharted 3's Cartagena market: about 4% more frames on the full CPU and about 4.5% with a slower CPU simulated (8 threads at 60%). The gain is in CPU-bound scenes; at native 3440x1440 with DLSS the frame rate is mostly limited by the graphics card and changes little. Your results will differ with your hardware.
